Focus Lock

Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 November 2026

Focus Lock locks your phone for a set time so you can focus. Only calls, SMS, emergency calls and any extra apps you pick before a lock stay usable. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything about your locks stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google AdMob collects to show ads.

1. Information that stays on your phone

Focus Lock stores the following only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is excluded from cloud backups:

  • The lock that is running: its length, start time and time left.
  • Your lock history: when each lock started and ended, how long it was, how many times a blocked app was opened, and how many notifications were held back.
  • The extra apps you picked (for example Google Maps or a music app).
  • Your Do Not Disturb settings from before a lock, so they can be put back afterwards.

We don't have servers, accounts or analytics of our own. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

2. Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhat it is used for
Accessibility serviceTo see which app is in front during a lock, so blocked apps can be covered. It reads only the app's name (package name). It does not read what is on your screen or what you type, and the app names it sees are never stored or sent anywhere. It does nothing when no lock is running.
Display over other appsTo show the lock screen over other apps during a lock.
Do Not Disturb accessTo let only calls ring during a lock, and to restore your own settings when it ends.
Notification accessTo hold back notifications from blocked apps until the lock ends (they are snoozed, not deleted) and to count new SMS. Notification content is not stored or sent.
Device adminOnly to stop the app being uninstalled while a lock is running. It uses no other device-admin powers. "Remove app" on the home screen turns it off at any time outside a lock.
Run at startupTo resume a lock after the phone restarts.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Focus Lock is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: one when you open the app and one after a lock finishes. No ads are shown or loaded while a lock is running.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location.
  • How you interact with ads and the app (for example ad views and taps, app launches).
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data of the ads SDK.

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy link on the app's home screen.
  • On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path varies by phone).

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information and we do not share any of the information in section 1 with anyone. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, through AdMob, as described in section 3.

5. Children

Focus Lock is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

Lock history stays on your phone until you uninstall the app or clear its data (Settings → Apps → Focus Lock → Storage). For data collected by Google AdMob, see Google's Privacy Policy linked above.

7. Security

App data is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read, and is excluded from backups. Ad traffic between your phone and Google is encrypted.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
